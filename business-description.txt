Our Core Services:

❤️ General Cardiac Consultation — Comprehensive evaluation of heart health, including detailed history, physical examination, and risk assessment. Dr. Gill takes time to listen to every patient, ensuring no symptom goes unnoticed.

❤️ Hypertension Management — High blood pressure is a silent killer. We offer personalized treatment plans including lifestyle modifications, dietary guidance, and medication management to keep your BP under control.

❤️ Diabetes & Heart Disease Management — Diabetes and heart disease go hand in hand. Our integrated approach addresses both conditions simultaneously for better long-term outcomes.

❤️ Chest Pain Evaluation — Immediate and thorough assessment of chest pain to rule out or confirm cardiac causes. We use evidence-based protocols to ensure rapid diagnosis.

❤️ Heart Failure Management — Comprehensive care for patients living with heart failure, including medication optimization, fluid management, and regular monitoring.

❤️ Cholesterol / Lipid Management — We help you manage your cholesterol levels through a combination of diet, exercise, and medication — reducing your risk of heart attack and stroke.

❤️ Preventive Cardiology — Our specialty! We strongly believe prevention is better than cure. We offer:
- Complete cardiac risk assessment
- BMI and obesity evaluation
- Framingham Risk Score calculation
- ASCVD risk scoring
- BSA (Body Surface Area) calculation
- Ideal weight assessment
- Personalized diet and exercise plans

❤️ ECG & Basic Cardiac Investigations — On-site basic cardiac diagnostic services to support accurate clinical decision-making.

❤️ Heart Health Education — We empower our patients with knowledge. Our website features heart health tips, diet plans, and a health blog to help you make informed decisions about your cardiovascular health.

❤️ Online Appointment Booking — Patients can now book appointments directly through our official website, saving time and avoiding long waiting queues.

Who Should Visit Us?

You should schedule an appointment at Gill Heart Clinic if you experience:
- Chest pain, pressure, or discomfort
- Shortness of breath
- Palpitations or irregular heartbeat
- Dizziness or fainting spells
- Swelling in feet or ankles
- High blood pressure
- High cholesterol
- Diabetes (especially with cardiac risk)
- Family history of heart disease
- Or if you simply want a routine heart check-up
