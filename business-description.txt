Gill Heart Clinic, under the leadership of Dr. Gurjeet Singh Gill — an experienced Cardio-Physician in Meerut with 15+ years of practice and 50,000+ patients treated — is a dedicated heart care clinic in Mohiuddinpur, Meerut. With specialized training in clinical cardiology and critical care (PGDCCP), Dr. Gill provides affordable, evidence-based cardiac care to patients across Meerut, Delhi NCR and Western Uttar Pradesh.

Our Core Services:

❤️ Cardiac Consultation (Heart Specialist Opinion) — Comprehensive heart health evaluation, second opinion, and ongoing cardiac follow-up. Dr. Gill takes time to listen to every patient so no symptom goes unnoticed.

❤️ Chest Pain & Angina Care — Evaluation and treatment of angina and heart attack warning signs, plus post-attack follow-up care.

❤️ Hypertension (High BP) Clinic — Personalized BP management with lifestyle modification, dietary guidance and medication planning.

❤️ Report Second Opinion — Expert review and second opinion on your ECG, 2D Echo, TMT and Holter reports, with a clear explanation of findings and the way forward.

❤️ General Medicine — Care for fever, weakness, infections and general health issues.

❤️ Respiratory Care — Assessment and care for cough, cold, asthma and breathing problems.

❤️ Digestive Health — Acidity, gas, indigestion and stomach health management.

❤️ Palpitations & Arrhythmia Care — Evaluation and management of irregular heartbeat and rhythm disorders.

❤️ Heart Failure Management — Comprehensive care including medication optimization, fluid management and regular monitoring.

❤️ Diabetes & Heart Care — Integrated management of diabetes and related heart risk.

❤️ Cholesterol (Lipid) Clinic — Cholesterol management through diet, exercise and medication to reduce heart attack and stroke risk.

❤️ Breathlessness Evaluation — Assessment of shortness of breath and lung-heart causes.

❤️ Preventive Healthcare — Cardiac risk assessment, BMI evaluation, Framingham & ASCVD risk scoring, plus preventive health planning.

❤️ Heart Health Check-up Packages — Complete cardiac check-ups for routine screening and high-risk patients.

❤️ Lifestyle & Weight Counseling — Personalized diet, exercise and heart-healthy lifestyle plans.

❤️ Critical Care Cardiology (PGDCCP) — Clinical cardiology and critical care expertise backed by Post Graduate Diploma in Clinical Cardiology & Critical Care.
