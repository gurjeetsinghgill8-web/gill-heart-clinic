❗️ Obesity aur Heart Risk Factors

Obesity aur heart risk factors kai tarah se jode huye hain. Jab aap apne vajan ko nahin keh pate hain, toh yah aapka dil aur daaman donon ko khatre mein daal sakta hai. Iske liye, aapko apne vajan ko kam karne aur swasth jeevan bharana hoga.

❓ Patient Question: 'Main kaise apne vajan ko kam kar sakta hoon?'

🩺 Dr. Gill's Answer: Aapko apne vajan ko kam karne ke liye ek healthy diet aur naitik jeevan shaili se judna hoga. Aapko apne khane mein kai tarah ke fruits, vegetables aur whole grains shamil karna chahiye. Sath hi, aapko apne exercise routine mein naye vyayam shamil karna chahiye, jaise ki yoga, swimming ya cycling. Apne vajan ko kam karne ke liye, aapko ek doctor ke saath kaam karne ka mukhya bhi hoga, jo aapko apne vajan ke liye ek nishkarsh plan banayega. Dr. Eugene Braunwald ke anusaar, 'Obesity aur heart disease donon hi ek dusre ke saath judhe huye hain.'

❓ Patient Question: 'Mujhe dil ki dard hota hai, kya yah obesity ke karan hai?'

🩺 Dr. Gill's Answer: Haa, yah sambhav hai ki aapke dil ki dard obesity ke karan ho. Jab aap apne vajan ko kam nahin keh pate hain, toh yah aapke dil par zyada pressure daal sakta hai, jo dil ki dard ki wajah ban sakta hai. Iske alawa, aapke shuruaat mein diabetes ya high blood pressure bhi ho sakte hain, jo dil ki dard ki wajah ban sakte hain. Lekin, aapko apne doctor ke saath milne ka mukhya hoga, jo aapko apne dil ki dard ke liye ek nishkarsh plan banayega. Dr. Valentin Fuster ke anusaar, 'Dil ki dard aur obesity donon hi ek dusre ke saath judhe huye hain.'

❓ Patient Question: 'Mujhe kaise pata chale ki main obesity aur heart risk factors se lad raha hoon?'

🩺 Dr. Gill's Answer: Aapko apne weight ke liye ek BMI (Body Mass Index) check karwana hoga. Yah aapke weight aur height ke beech ka ristaa hota hai. Agar aapka BMI 30 se adhik hai, toh aap obesity se lad rahe hain. Iske alawa, aapko apne doctor ke saath blood pressure check karwana chahiye aur apne cholesterol ke liye ek checkup karwana chahiye. Dr. Deepak L. Bhatt ke anusaar, 'Obesity aur heart disease donon hi ek dusre ke saath judhe huye hain.'

❓ Patient Question: 'Mujhe kaise pata chale ki main apne vajan ko kam kar sakta hoon?'

🩺 Dr. Gill's Answer: Aapko apne vajan ko kam karne ke liye ek doctor ke saath kaam karne ka mukhya hoga. Aapke doctor aapko ek nishkarsh plan banayega, jo aapke vajan ko kam karne ke liye sahi khane aur vyayam ki jagah dikhayega.
