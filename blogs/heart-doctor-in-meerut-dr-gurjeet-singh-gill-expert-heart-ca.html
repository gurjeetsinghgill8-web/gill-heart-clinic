🩺 Patient Questions & Answers

❓ Patient Question: "Doctor sahab, mujhe kabhi kabhi chhati mein dard hota hai, kya yeh heart problem hai?"

🩺 Dr. Gill's Answer: Yeh bahut common sawal hai. Chhati ka dard hamesha heart ka nahi hota – gas, muscle pain, ya anxiety bhi ho sakta hai. Lekin heart attack ka dard aksar chhati ke beech mein dabav ya jalne jaisa hota hai, jo baaye haath, jabde, ya peeth tak phail sakta hai. Agar aapko saans phoolna, paseena aana, ya chakkar aaye, toh turant emergency mein jayen. Framingham Heart Study ke mutabik, chhati ka dard heart disease ka ek important symptom hai, lekin iska matlab yeh nahi ki har dard heart ka hai. Isliye, kisi bhi naye ya badhte dard ke liye ECG aur doctor ki salah zaroori hai. Hum Gill Heart Clinic mein aapka poora check-up karte hain – ECG, ECHO, stress test – taaki sahi diagnosis ho sake. Yaad rakhein, der karne se heart ko nuksan ho sakta hai.

❓ Patient Question: "Mujhe high BP hai, kya yeh heart attack ka khatra badhata hai?"

🩺 Dr. Gill's Answer: Bilkul! High blood pressure (hypertension) ko 'silent killer' kaha jata hai kyunki ismein aksar koi lakshan nahi dikhte. SPRINT Trial (2015) ne dikhaya ki BP ko 120 se neeche rakhne se heart attack, stroke, aur heart failure ka khatra kaafi kam ho jata hai. BP ki wajah se aapki arteries par dabav badhta hai, jisse wo sakht ho jati hain aur plaque jamne lagta hai. Indian patients mein namak ka zyada sevan, stress, aur family history common risk factors hain. Main hamesha kehta hoon – BP ki dawai roz lena, namak kam karna, aur regular exercise karna. PM Jan Aushadhi Kendra se milne wali generic dawaiyan (jaise amlodipine, telmisartan) bilkul asar karti hain aur sasti bhi hain. Agar aapka BP control nahi hai, toh jald se jald apne heart doctor se milein.

❓ Patient Question: "Doctor, mujhe diabetes hai, toh kya main heart disease se bach sakta hoon?"

🩺 Dr. Gill's Answer: Diabetes aur heart disease ka gehrisambandh hai. High sugar levels aapki blood vessels ko nuksan pahunchati hain, jisse blockages jaldi banti hain. ISCHEMIA Trial (2020) ne bataya ki diabetes wale patients mein aggressive risk factor control (sugar, BP, cholesterol) utna hi zaroori hai jitna angioplasty ya bypass. Isliye main apne diabetes patients ko salah deta hoon – HbA1c ko 7% se neeche rakhein, roz 30 minute tez chalna, aur apni diet mein refined sugar aur maida kam karein. Dr. Deepak L. Bhatt, ek renowned cardiologist, ne bhi is baat par zor diya hai ki diabetes mein heart ko bachane ke liye holistic approach chahiye. Gill Heart Clinic mein hum aapke sugar, cholesterol, aur BP ko milke control karte hain, taaki aapka heart safe rahe.

❓ Patient Question: "Kya cholesterol kam karne ke liye dawai ki zaroorat hai ya sirf diet se kaam chalega?"

🩺 Dr. Gill's Answer: Yeh sawal bahut aaya hai. Agar aapka LDL (bad cholesterol) bahut high hai (160 se upar) ya aapko pehle se heart disease hai, toh sirf diet se kaam nahi chalega. American Heart Association (AHA) guidelines ke mutabik, high-risk patients ko statin dawai (jaise atorvastatin) lena zaroori hai. Diet aur exercise zaroor madad karti hai, lekin dawai ke saath. PM Jan Aushadhi se milne wali statins bilkul effective hain aur budget-friendly bhi. Main apne patients ko kehta hoon – gharelu upay (jaise lahsun, methi) saath mein le sakte hain, par dawai band nahi karni chahiye. Pehle apna lipid profile test karwayein, phir doctor ki salah se dawai shuru karein.

❓ Patient Question: "Mujhe saans phoolti hai, kya yeh heart failure ka lakshan ho sakta hai?"

🩺 Dr. Gill's Answer: Saans phoolna (shortness of breath) heart failure ka common symptom hai, especially jab aap lete hain ya kam mehnat par bhi phoolti hai. Heart failure ka matlab hai ki dil ka pump kamzor ho gaya hai. Dr. Eugene Braunwald, jinhone heart failure par kaafi research ki, unhone bataya ki early diagnosis aur treatment se quality of life behtar ho sakti hai. Indian patients mein high BP aur diabetes ki wajah se heart failure aam hai. Isliye, agar aapko yeh problem ho rahi hai, toh ECHO test zaroor karwayein – yeh dil ke pump function ko check karta hai. Gill Heart Clinic mein hum heart failure ka modern treatment karte hain, jisse aapki saans ki takleef kam ho aur aap normal life jee sakein.

❓ Patient Question: "Kya heart attack ke baad normal life jee sakta hoon?"

🩺 Dr. Gill's Answer: Bilkul! Heart attack ke baad bhi aap lambi aur active zindagi jee sakte hain, agar aap dawaiyan niyamit lein, lifestyle change karein, aur cardiac rehabilitation mein bhaag lein. Dr. Valentin Fuster, ek world-renowned cardiologist, ka kehna hai ki heart attack ke baad patient ki apni dekhbhal sabse important hai. Ismein dawaiyan (aspirin, statin, beta-blocker), healthy diet, stress management, aur dheere-dheere exercise shamil hai. Main apne patients ko hamesha encourage karta hoon – apna weight control karein, dhumrapan chhodein, aur roz 30 minute tez chalein. Gill Heart Clinic mein hum aapke follow-up ka poora dhyan rakhte hain, taaki aapka heart strong rahe.

🥗 Indian Diet & Lifestyle Tips for a Healthy Heart

  • Khaana: Desi ghee ka kam istemal karein, tel meetha (mustard) ya olive oil use karein. Roti gehun ki, dal, sabzi, aur fresh fruit khayen. Namak kam (5gm/day se kam), sugar aur maida bilkul avoid karein.
  • Exercise: Roz 30-45 minute tez chalna, cycling, ya yoga karein. Din mein 10,000 steps ka target rakhein.
  • Stress: Meditation ya pranayam se stress kam karein. Family ke saath time bitayein.
  • Dawai: PM Jan Aushadhi se generic dawaiyan len, yeh sasti aur asardar hoti hain. Bina doctor ke salah ke dawai band na karein.
  • Nind: Roz 7-8 ghanta soyein.

❓ FAQ

1. Kya heart check-up ke liye fasting zaroori hai?

Haan, blood test (sugar, cholesterol) ke liye 8-12 ghante fasting zaroori hai. ECG ya ECHO ke liye fasting ki zaroorat nahi hai.

2. Kya heart disease bachon ko ho sakti hai?

Bachon mein aam taur par congenital heart disease ho sakti hai, jo janm se hoti hai. Iska alag se ilaj hota hai. Adult mein heart disease aksar lifestyle se hoti hai.

3. Kya main heart attack ke baad ghar par aaram kar sakta hoon?

Nahi, heart attack ke baad pehle 1-2 din hospital mein observation zaroori hai. Doctor ki salah ke baad hi ghar jayen aur follow-up karein.

4. Kya heart patient ko aloo khana chahiye?

Aloo mein starch hota hai, isliye kam khayen. Agar khana hai, toh boiled ya roasted karein, deep-fried nahi. Isse sugar aur weight control rahega.
