❓ Patient Question: "Doctor sahab, mujhe kab heart doctor ke paas jaana chahiye? Kya symptoms hain jo ignore nahi karne chahiye?"

🩺 Dr. Gill's Answer: Yeh bahut important sawaal hai. Aapko heart doctor se turant milna chahiye agar aapko yeh symptoms hain: seene mein dard ya bhaaripan (jo haath, kandhe, jaw ya pet tak phail sakta hai), saans phoolna (khaaskar chadhne ya chalne par), chakkar aana, bahut zyada thakaan, dhadkan ka tez ya irregular hona, aur paon ya pairon mein sujaan. Par dhyan rakhein – kai baar heart attack ke symptoms atypical hote hain, jaise pet ke upar dard, ya sirf bahut zyada nausea. Agar aapko diabetes hai, high BP hai, ya smoking karte hain, toh aapko aur careful rehna chahiye. World Heart Federation ke mutabik, heart disease ko early pakadne se heart attack ka khatra 50% tak kam ho sakta hai. Agar aapko koi bhi doubt ho, toh bina jhijhak check-up karwayein. Gill Heart Clinic mein hum ECG, echo, aur TMT jaisi basic tests karte hain, jisse heart ki condition ka pata chalta hai.

❓ Patient Question: "Mujhe high BP hai, kya yeh heart attack ka khatra badhata hai?"

🩺 Dr. Gill's Answer: Bilkul! High blood pressure (hypertension) ko 'silent killer' kaha jaata hai kyunki isme aksar koi symptom nahi hota, lekin yeh aapke arteries ko damage karta hai. Framingham Heart Study, jo decades purani hai, ne clearly dikhaya hai ki uncontrolled BP heart attack, stroke, aur heart failure ka biggest risk factor hai. SPRINT Trial (2015) mein bhi yeh proof hua ki BP ko 120 systolic se kam rakhne par heart problems mein significant kami aati hai, balki 60+ umar ke logon mein bhi. Main apne patients ko advise karta hoon ki BP regular check karein, aur agar doctor ne medicine di hai toh usko regularly lein. PM Jan Aushadhi Kendra se generic amlodipine, telmisartan, ya losartan bahut sasti milti hai – quality bilkul wahi hai jo branded ki hoti hai. BP ko control karna heart attack se bachne ka sabse asaan tarika hai.

❓ Patient Question: "Meri diet mein kya change karu? Main Punjabi hoon, aur parathe aur butter bahut pasand hai."

🩺 Dr. Gill's Answer: Yeh common problem hai! Parathe, butter, aur ghee ka zyada use cholesterol badhata hai, jo arteries mein plaque bana kar heart attack ka karan banta hai. Iska matlab yeh nahi ki aap apna favourite khana chhod dein. Bas kuch smart changes karein: 1) Ghee/butter ki jagah mustard oil ya olive oil use karein (2-3 tbsp daily). 2) Parathe mein aata gehun ka use karein aur usme besan ya moong dal bhar kar healthy banaayein. 3) Sabziyon mein namak kam karein, aur achaar/chatni se parhez karein. 4) Roti, chawal, aur dal ka ratio sahi rakhein – aadhi plate sabzi, chauthai dal, chauthai carb. 5) Fruits jaise apple, papaya, aur amla zaroor khayein. American Heart Association (AHA) kehta hai ki diet mein soluble fiber (jaise oats, bajra) cholesterol kam karta hai. Aur haan, processed food, maida, aur sugary drinks (cold drink) bilkul avoid karein – yeh heart ke liye poison hain. Ek din mein ek cheez kam karke bhi bada farak padta hai.

❓ Patient Question: "Kya stress aur tension se heart attack ho sakta hai?"

🩺 Dr. Gill's Answer: Haan, bilkul! Chronic stress se body mein cortisol aur adrenaline badhta hai, jisse BP aur heart rate upar jaata hai, aur over time arteries ko nuksan pahunchta hai. INTERHEART Study (jisme 52 countries ke logon ne hissa liya) ne dikhaya ki psychological stress heart attack ka major risk factor hai. Isliye main apne patients ko stress management sikhata hoon – pranayam (deep breathing) roz 10 minute karein, 30 minute ki tez walk karein, aur apne shauk (music, gardening) ke liye waqt nikalein. Aur haan, achhi neend bahut zaroori hai – 7-8 ghante. Agar aapko lagta hai ki stress control nahi ho raha, toh doctor se baat karein. Isme koi shame nahi hai. Dil aur dimaag dono ka khayal rakhna zaroori hai.

❓ Patient Question: "Mujhe diabetes hai, toh kya heart disease ka khatra zyada hai?"

🩺 Dr. Gill's Answer: Ji haan, diabetes (khaaskar type 2) heart disease ka risk 2-4 guna badha deta hai. High sugar levels arteries ki inner lining (endothelium) ko damage karte hain, jisse plaque jaldi banta hai. Isliye diabetes ko strictly control mein rakhna heart attack se bachne ke liye sabse important hai. Aapko HbA1c test karwana chahiye (target <7%), aur BP bhi control mein hona chahiye (target <130/80). Main apne diabetic patients ko statins (jaise atorvastatin) bhi deta hoon, kyunki AHA/ACC guidelines ke mutabik diabetes mein cholesterol kam karna zaroori hai, chahe cholesterol normal ho. PM Jan Aushadhi se metformin aur atorvastatin dono saste milte hain. Aur haan, regular foot check aur eye check-up bhi karte rahein, kyunki diabetes poore body ko affect karta hai.

❓ Patient Question: "Kya heart attack ke baad normal life possible hai?"

🩺 Dr. Gill's Answer: Bilkul! Aaj kal heart attack ke baad bhi log 100% normal life jee sakte hain, agar sahi treatment aur lifestyle follow karein. ISCHEMIA Trial (2020) ne dikhaya ki angioplasty ya bypass ke bina bhi, medicines aur lifestyle changes se results utne hi achhe ho sakte hain – lekin yeh decision doctor ko case-by-case basis par lena chahiye. Main apne patients ko cardiac rehabilitation program suggest karta hoon – jisme dheere-dheere exercise, diet counselling, aur stress management hota hai. Heart attack ke baad aapko dawaiyan (aspirin, clopidogrel, statin) regular lena hain, aur follow-up par aana hai. Main khud aise kai patients ko dekhta hoon jo heart attack ke baad bhi 10-20 saal tak healthy life jee rahe hain. Toh himmat rakhin, aur doctor ke bataye hue rules follow karein.

Indian-Specific Diet and Lifestyle Tips by Dr. Gill

Yahan kuch practical tips hain jo main apne har patient ko deta hoon:

  • Ghee aur butter ka sevan limit karein – ek din mein 1-2 tsp ghee kaafi hai.
  • Namak kam karein – khana banate waqt namak kam daalein, aur achaar/sauces avoid karein.
  • Subah ki walk zaroor karein – 30-40 minute tez walk, hafte mein 5 din.
  • Roti mein gehun aur jowar/bajra mix karein – fiber badhega.
  • Fried snacks (samosa, pakora) ki jagah roasted chana ya makhana khayein.
  • Doodh, dahi, aur paneer ka moderate use – full-fat ki jagah low-fat better hai.
  • Raat ka khana 7 baje tak karein – late night eating heart par bura asar daalti hai.
  • Smoking aur paan masala bilkul chhod dein – yeh sabse bada risk factor hai.

In chhote changes se bada fark padta hai. Aur haan, PM Jan Aushadhi Kendra se generic medicines lein – yeh sasti aur asardar hain.

FAQ

❓ Kya main heart doctor ko online consult kar sakta hoon?

❓ Heart check-up mein kya tests hote hain?

Basic check-up mein ECG, blood pressure, lipid profile (cholesterol), blood sugar, aur BMI shamil hai. Agar zaroorat ho toh echo (ultrasound of heart) aur TMT (treadmill test) bhi karte hain.

❓ Kya generic medicines branded jitni hi asardar hain?

Bilkul! Generic medicines ka active ingredient wahi hota hai, aur quality bhi same hoti hai (USFDA-approved). PM Jan Aushadhi Kendra par yeh 50-90% sasti milti hain. Main khud apne patients ko generic dawaiyan hi suggest karta hoon.

❓ Kya heart attack se pehle koi warning signs milte hain?

Haan, kai baar milete hain – jaise baar-baar seene mein dard jo aata jaata hai (angina), thakaan, saans phoolna, ya pet ke upar bechaini. Agar aapko yeh symptoms hain, toh turant doctor se milein.
