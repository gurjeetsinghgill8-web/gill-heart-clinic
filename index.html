About Dr. Gurjeet Singh Gill (Dr. GS Gill)

Dr. Gurjeet Singh Gill (Dr. GS Gill) is a highly experienced Cardio-Physician with 15+ years of clinical expertise in non-invasive cardiology and critical care. He is currently bridging the gap between medicine and technology through an advanced certification in Artificial Intelligence in Healthcare from IIT Kanpur.

Dr. Gill holds his MBBS from Govt Medical College MPSMC (through All India PMT) and completed his Diploma in Cardiology at UN Mehta Institute of Cardiology, Ahmedabad, Gujarat, along with PGDCCP (NI). He has a unique blend of deep clinical domain knowledge and practical skills in Generative AI, Prompt Engineering, and No-Code Development. He is passionate about developing patient-centric AI agents and automating clinical workflows.

Mission: Quality Heart Treatment for Every Patient — regardless of their financial background.

Heart Health Tips

Simple daily habits for a healthy heart — from Dr G S Gill.

Walk 30 Min Daily

Brisk walking for 30 minutes reduces heart disease risk by 35%. Start today!

Eat Colorful

5 servings of fruits & vegetables daily. Green, red, yellow — eat the rainbow!

Cut The Salt

Less than 5g salt per day. High BP patients: avoid pickles, papad, and processed food.

Quit Tobacco

Smokers have 2-4x higher heart attack risk. Quit today — your heart will thank you.

Sleep 7-8 Hours

Poor sleep increases BP and stress hormones. Fix your sleep schedule for heart health.

Regular Checkup

Yearly heart checkup after 35. ECG + BP + Sugar — get a complete checkup at Gill Heart Clinic!
